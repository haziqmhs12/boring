What I want to learn next

  • Site-to-site IPsec tunnels with dynamic routing between HQ and the two branches, with MTU and MSS tuning.
  • NAT/PAT at branch edges and firewall policy between VLAN zones, including blocking guest and IoT traffic from servers.
  • Centralised AAA, NTP, SNMP and syslog, then 802.1X with dynamic VLAN assignment.
  • Wireless: controller and access point deployment for the guest and staff networks.
  • Configuration backup and firmware upgrade procedures, and automation with Python.
